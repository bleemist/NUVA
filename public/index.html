The Ugandan PUJAB Weight Formula

Understanding how UACE grade points translate into university admissions weight.

Official Public Universities Joint Admissions Board Formula

Total Weight = (Essential × 3) + (Relevant × 2) + Subsidiary + O-Level Weight + Gender Bonus

1. Principal Grade Scale

  • A = 6 points
  • B = 5 points
  • C = 4 points
  • D = 3 points
  • E = 2 points
  • O = 1 point (Subsidiary Pass)
  • F = 0 points (Fail)

2. Subject Multipliers

  • Essential Subjects (Top 2): Grade points × 3
  • Relevant / Desirable: Grade points × 2
  • General Paper (GP): +1.0 point on pass
  • Other Subsidiary (ICT/Maths): +1.0 point on pass

3. Bonus & O-Level Points

  • O-Level Weight: 0 to 3.0 points derived from distinction/credit counts.
  • Female Affirmative Action: +1.5 points automatically awarded to all female applicants across Ugandan public universities.

Practical Calculation Example

Consider a female student offering Physics (A), Mathematics (B), Technical Drawing (A) with a General Paper pass, Sub-Maths pass, and O-Level weight of 2.4 applying for Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering at Makerere University:

✓ Mathematics (B): 5 points × 3 (Essential) = 15.0
✓ Physics (A): 6 points × 3 (Essential) = 18.0
✓ Technical Drawing (A): 6 points × 2 (Relevant) = 12.0
✓ General Paper (Pass): +1.0
✓ Other Subsidiary (Pass): +1.0
✓ O-Level Weight: +2.4
✓ Female Gender Bonus: +1.5
Total Calculated Admission Weight: 15.0 + 18.0 + 12.0 + 1.0 + 1.0 + 2.4 + 1.5 = 50.9 points
