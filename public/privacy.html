Transparency & Security

Privacy Policy & Data Ethics

Last updated: March 2026 · BLEEM Labs Co.

1. Information We Collect

NUVA operates on a strict Privacy-First & Anonymous architecture. We do not require any account registration, email signups, phone numbers, or passwords to access course guidance.

When you use our course recommendation tool, the academic information you input (gender, education level, UCE aggregates, and A-Level subjects/grades) is processed in real time and cached temporarily in your browser's client-side sessionStorage. Closing your browser tab automatically flushes this data.

2. Purpose of Processing

Your inputs are processed solely to:

  • Calculate your official PUJAB admission weight according to UNEB formulas.
  • Match your calculated weight against public university cut-off points.
  • Present your personalized recommendations deck on screen.

We never sell, rent, monetize, or transmit your individual academic grades to third parties or recruiters.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

NUVA uses browser session storage to pass calculation results seamlessly between the input form and the results deck without storing data on a database server. No tracking or invasive cross-site cookies are installed by our core system.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

We display advertisements through Google AdSense to sustain free server hosting and guidance services for Ugandan youth. Google may use non-personalized cookies to serve relevant ads. You can review Google's advertising policies at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Your Rights & Data Freedom

You hold full ownership of your data. You can clear all cached academic records at any time by clearing your browser cache or restarting your browser.
