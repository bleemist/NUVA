The NUVA Vision

Empowering Ugandan Students Through Technology

NUVA is Uganda's premier AI-powered course guidance expert system, revolutionizing how A-Level leavers find their ideal university courses across Makerere, Kyambogo, MUST, and all 10 public universities.

Our Story

The idea for NUVA was born in 2025 at Kyambogo University when founder MUYANJA ANDREW witnessed firsthand the distress students faced during tertiary applications. Many students had exceptional grades but didn't know how to calculate their official PUJAB admission weights, or missed out on dream courses because they lacked accessible information on cut-off points.

What began as a specialized algorithm developed by BLEEM Labs Co. has rapidly evolved into a trusted nationwide platform, helping thousands of secondary school leavers, parents, and educators make informed academic choices.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To democratize university course guidance across Uganda by providing an accurate, completely free, and instantaneous admissions matching platform.

Vision: To ensure no Ugandan student is left behind or misplaced due to informational barriers, empowering the next generation of engineers, doctors, teachers, scientists, and leaders.

Why the Name "NUVA"?

The name is derived from Nova (a bright new star) combined with U for Uganda. NUVA represents a guiding star illuminating the educational pathway for Ugandan students transitioning from secondary to tertiary education.

Development Journey

📌 2025: Conceptualization & Prototyping

Mathematical modeling of the Joint Admissions Board weighting system at Kyambogo University.

📌 January 2026: Beta Testing

NUVA prototype deployed and rigorously tested across 100+ subject combinations with Ugandan students.

📌 2026: Nationwide Deployment

Full deployment covering all 10 chartered public universities with 449+ undergraduate degree programs.
